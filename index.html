Revivo privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. Applies to Revivo – Smart Recovery (com.revivo.recovery), published by Playmax.

Revivo helps you find and restore deleted or hidden photos, videos, audio and documents on your phone, keep safety copies of new photos, back up contacts and keep files in a private vault. This page explains what the app accesses, what leaves your device and the choices you have.

Files and storage access

To scan for recoverable files, Revivo asks for access to your photos, videos and audio and, on newer Android versions, “All files access”. This access is used only to scan storage on your device, show previews and restore the files you choose. Scanning, previews, recovery, safety copies and the vault all run on your device. Your files are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Recovery results depend on your device and on how much has been overwritten since a file was deleted; not every file can be recovered.

Contacts

If you turn on Contact Time Machine and allow contacts access, Revivo saves snapshots of your contacts in the app's private storage so it can find and restore contacts that go missing. Contacts are read and written only on your device and are never uploaded.

Notifications

Revivo may show notifications, for example when a scan finishes or a safety copy is made. You can turn them off in Android settings.

Data deletion

Everything Revivo stores (scan results, safety copies, vault files, contact snapshots and settings) is kept in the app's private storage. You can delete items inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. There is no account and no server copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information and how you interact with ads, under its own privacy policy. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first using Google's consent form. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; we receive only the purchase status needed to unlock features, never your card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your files, photos, messages or contacts.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Ad partners do not receive your files, contacts or name.

Children

Revivo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Partner policies
